# ReOculus - 3 design directions

**Picked 2026-11-12: C Dark VR Neon, blue instead of purple.**

The layout is the same in all three, only the look changes, so
picking later is easy. The app code already supports all three and you can
preview each one live in Setup, Design. A and B below are unchanged;
only C was re-coloured blue, and C-blue is now the default.

All three mockups below are AI-generated concept images of the Home screen,
not screenshots of the running app. A few small labels in the images are
invented by the image generator (for example "Open Quest Link" in A, and
version numbers in B and C). Ignore those details, judge the look: colors,
brightness, cards, and feel.

## A. Clean Dark Pro

- Look: dark charcoal background, soft white text, one calm blue accent.
- Cards: quiet, flat, spaced out. Nothing shouts.
- Feels like: a proper professional desktop tool.
- Best for: everyday use, long sessions, easy on the eyes at night.
- Mockup: `design-options/media-generation-design-a-pro-dark-dashboard-0-29775681-41d1-4ec3-ac0a-560efd9aa238.webp`

![Design A, Clean Dark Pro](sandbox://workspace/quest-companion/flutter_app/design-options/media-generation-design-a-pro-dark-dashboard-0-29775681-41d1-4ec3-ac0a-560efd9aa238.webp)

## B. Light Friendly Simple

- Look: white and very light grey background, dark text, large type,
  blue main buttons, green Ready chip.
- Cards: white with soft shadows, lots of white space, big targets.
- Feels like: friendly, bright, the clearest to read.
- Best for: first-time users, bright rooms, anyone who wants the
  easiest reading.
- Mockup: `design-options/media-generation-design-b-light-friendly-dashbo-0-c8cefbde-990f-4919-ad66-1a7149ca5ad5.webp`

![Design B, Light Friendly Simple](sandbox://workspace/quest-companion/flutter_app/design-options/media-generation-design-b-light-friendly-dashbo-0-c8cefbde-990f-4919-ad66-1a7149ca5ad5.webp)

## C. Dark VR Neon — PICKED, blue instead of purple

- Look: near-black background, glowing bright blue and cyan accents,
  rounded cards with a soft blue glow around the headset card.
- Cards: dark blue, thin glowing blue borders.
- Feels like: a headset app, energetic, fun to show people.
- Best for: evening use, people who like the VR look.
- Mockup (original, purple version — the code theme is now blue):
  `design-options/media-generation-design-c-vr-neon-dashboard-0-097314b1-03cc-4110-bf22-11682a1f6088.webp`

headset card from C", or "A but bigger text like B". Small batches:

in small batches for your approval, not one big surprise at the end.
